NPCVO v0.1.1 FOUNDATION PATCH
==============================

- Replaced append-index-only dialogue capture with row identity + source-text tracking.
  This still catches vanilla DFU appended question/answer pairs and also tolerates custom
  TalkWindow implementations that rebuild or reuse ListBox rows.
- Added optional progressive NPC answer reveal (enabled by default). The original DFU
  answer string is never replaced, so logbook/history behavior keeps the complete line.
- Progressive text follows actual WAV playback position. Kokoro does not currently provide
  word timestamps, so synchronization is approximate and character-based with a small lag.
- Restores native dialogue immediately on interruption, failed synthesis/load, TalkWindow
  close, or manual stop.
- Added Player.log stage diagnostics for capture, synthesis, WAV readiness, and playback.
- Added Mod Settings toggle: General > Reveal NPC text with speech.

Validation performed in this workspace:
- modsettings.json parses successfully.
- NPCVO.dfmod.json parses successfully.
- C# delimiter/string/comment structural scan passes.
- Referenced ListBox, TextLabel, Panel, and component-collection APIs were checked against
  the Daggerfall Unity 1.1.1 source.

Not performed here:

- In-game runtime test.
