NPCVO v0.1.2

- Adds capture for DaggerfallQuestOfferWindow quest-giver text.
- Detects QuestOffer using UIWindowFactory registration, with a type-name fallback for compatibility.
- Handles QuestOffer windows that present their text through a child DaggerfallMessageBox.
- Extracts formatted MultiFormatTextLabel rows as well as ordinary text labels.
- Excludes button/player-choice captions from quest-offer speech.
- Waits for quest-offer text to remain stable across two polls before synthesis.
- Resolves quest-offer NPC data from the QuestOffer window's questorNPC/socialGroup rather than stale TalkManager state.
- Adds Source=TalkWindow / Source=QuestOffer to NPC capture diagnostics.
- Retains v0.1.1 robust TalkWindow row scanning and progressive TalkWindow text reveal.
- Progressive reveal is still limited to TalkWindow rows in this build.
