NPCVO v0.1.3.2 - VoiceTypes self-repair hotfix

- Fixes VoiceTypes.json regenerating/remaining as a top-level [] on affected installs.
- VoiceTypes now repairs [], {}, blank, malformed, and missing-profile files automatically.
- Built-in profiles are merged back without deleting custom profiles.
- Profile JSON is written with a deterministic serializer rather than relying on Unity JsonUtility for initial output.
- npcvo_test now hard-falls back to bm_george so it can test Kokoro even if voice assignment is broken.
- npcvo_status now reports VoiceTypes, UniqueVoices, and PortraitVoices profile counts.
