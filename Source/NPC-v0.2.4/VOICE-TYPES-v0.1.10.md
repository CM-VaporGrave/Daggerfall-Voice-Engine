# NPCVO Voice Types v0.1.10

Built-in VoiceTypes use schema v6.

## Redguard men

Redguard male profiles retain NPCVO's normal human US/UK voice pool, but borrow the disciplined baritone processing shape used for Orc men at reduced intensity: approximately -1.45 semitones, slightly slower cadence, moderate compression/resonance, and restrained gravel.

## Orc men

Male Orcs are nudged deeper than v0.1.9 (approximately -2.65 semitones) with slightly stronger compression, gravel, and throat resonance. Their dedicated curated Orc pool remains unchanged.
