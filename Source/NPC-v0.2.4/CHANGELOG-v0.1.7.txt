NPCVO v0.1.7
=============

- Adds optional emotional-delivery presets with Subtle/Default/Strong strength.
- Reads Dynamic Portraits Win/Loss and Happy/Angry state when Emotion Sync is enabled.
- Adds conservative grammar/punctuation polish for Kokoro delivery while preserving original DFU text for logic and VoicePack hashes.
- Adds curated SpecialVoices story-character/Daedric fallback layer.
- Adds spectral + short reverb DSP to shared Kokoro v1.3; Daedric profiles use it by default.
- Adds canonical pre-generated character pack layout: VoicePack/Characters/<slug>/Lines/<hash>.wav.
- Adds SpecialVoices.default.json, special-character research notes, and pre-gen manifest/folder scaffolding.
- Keeps legacy VoicePack paths and all v0.1.6 procedural VoiceTypes intact.
