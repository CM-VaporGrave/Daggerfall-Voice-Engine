DAGGERFALL NARRATOR - PLAYER v0.3.7 BUILD / RELEASE CHECKLIST

1. Use the Daggerfall Unity 1.1.1 source project in Unity 2019.4.40f1.
2. Copy Assets/Game/Mods/PlayerVO into the matching DFU source path.
3. Let Unity compile and confirm there are zero red Console errors.
4. Build PlayerVO.dfmod with Daggerfall Tools > Mod Builder using the normal Precompiled workflow.
5. Install PlayerVO.dfmod under DaggerfallUnity_Data/StreamingAssets/Mods/.
6. Install Daggerfall Voice Engine under DaggerfallUnity_Data/StreamingAssets/DaggerfallVoiceEngine/.

CHARACTER CREATION
7. Start a new character and finish biography Q&A. Confirm the personality picker appears at the CreateCharSummary hand-off even before PlayerEntity exists.
8. Hover/focus several signs. Confirm the left highlight and right preview remain synchronized, the preview uses constellation-style star art, and the bio reads as backstory/personality rather than a mechanical power.
9. Select The Apprentice and confirm the archetype subtitle is Agent and its bio describes a newcomer to the land: quiet, learning, mission-focused, and sparing with words.
10. Select a personality and enter the game. Confirm no personality label/button clips the vanilla Summary skill/reflex UI.

VOICE IN CHARACTER CREATION
11. On CreateCharSummary, confirm Personality cannot be dismissed with Back/Escape and Use Personality returns cleanly to Summary before Voice opens.
12. Press DFU Restart from Summary, repeat chargen, and confirm Personality appears again before Voice instead of skipping directly to Voice.
13. Confirm Accent All/US/UK and Gender All/Female/Male only filter the packaged list; All/All must expose every .npy voice physically present in voices/ and voices-custom/.
14. Confirm the initial Gender filter follows the new character when available, then deliberately switch filters and select a voice of any gender/accent.
15. Cycle several packaged voices, adjust Depth, Speed and Clean/CD-ROM/DOS quality, and press Preview Voice after each change. Confirm the audible result matches the displayed settings.
16. Press Use Voice, enter the game, and confirm the first bark uses that exact voice/profile.

VOICE / CHARACTER PERSISTENCE
11. Choose a Player voice profile, depth, speed, and Clean/CD-ROM/DOS style. Save, switch to another character with different voice settings, then switch back and confirm each character restores its own voice profile.
12. Set Voice Override to an installed exact Engine voice ID (for example am_granite). Confirm it takes precedence over Accent/Gender/Delivery; clear the field and confirm normal selectors resume.
13. Confirm US + Male + Rugged resolves to am_granite.
14. Generate the same short line in Clean, CD-ROM, and DOS modes and confirm fresh cache entries are used and the quality treatments are audibly distinct.

DIALOGUE / PERSONALITY
15. Accept and reject quest-giver prompts with several personalities. Confirm the underlying DFU choice remains unchanged while PlayerVO voices a personality-authored response rather than generic Yes/No.
16. With Agent selected, confirm deliberate quest choices are concise but voiced; ambient routed observations should normally fall back to Dungeon Master.
17. Route Dungeon Master Observation Voice to Player, look at an object/creature, and confirm non-Agent personalities speak in first person with personality variation while preserving the observed subject.
18. Test manual bark before and after NPC Q&A and after loading a different character. Confirm Player/NPC/DM speech continues and no stale Voice Engine turn remains.
