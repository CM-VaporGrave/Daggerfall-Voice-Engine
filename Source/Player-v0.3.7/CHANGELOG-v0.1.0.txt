PlayerVO v0.1.0

- Initial Kokoro Voice Framework player-character module.
- Player-selectable Kokoro voice with in-settings and console sound tests.
- Contextual automatic barks plus manual context bark / keyboard speech / hybrid bark-key modes.
- Race-flavored authored lines and seven personality profiles.
- Optional Action Movie mode with context-matched movie-reference one-liners and lore-friendly variants.
- Vanilla damage/exertion/death vocal priority and playback-time lockout re-checks to prevent voice collisions.
- Health, fatigue, magicka, breath, recovery, and encumbrance threshold reactions.
- Reflection-only Climates & Calories integration.
- Structural quest awareness without prose interpretation or an LLM.
- Safe authored semantic responses for recognized quest/court choice intents.
- Soft NPCVO bridge for player portrait + typewritten/spoken turns in enhanced quest presentation; vanilla DFU UI remains the fallback.
- Public NotifyContext() hook for precise integrations such as FrostKill/FireKill.
- Editable Barks.json and rolling speech cache.
