# Player personality grimoire

Voice and personality are independent settings. Personality controls authored worldview only.

## Mirror pairs
- The Mage (Gandalf): heroic wisdom / responsible knowledge ↔ The Ritual (Voldemort): forbidden power / occult obsession.
- The Lady (Padmé): diplomacy / stewardship ↔ The Lord (Kain, Legacy of Kain): domination / regal superiority.
- The Warrior (Aragorn): honor / direct duty ↔ The Thief (Garrett, darker): unfair advantage / self-preservation.
- The Lover (Samwise): compassion / loyalty ↔ The Serpent (Joker): cruelty / malice / chaos.
- The Steed (Indiana Jones): adventure / discovery ↔ The Tower (Belloq): possession / prestige / acquisition.
- The Atronach (Geralt + 007): calm professional competence ↔ The Shadow (Man with No Name / bounty hunter): suspicious pursuit, tracking, ambush awareness.

## Center
- The Apprentice: **Agent**. New to the Iliac Bay and still learning its customs, the Agent is dedicated to the larger quest and economical with words. Ambient chatter is sparse, while deliberate dialogue and important quest/danger reactions remain available.

## Resolver philosophy
Personality determines why the character cares. Context determines what is happening. Lore profile determines what the archetype plausibly knows. Race supplies cultural perspective. Main-quest-specific knowledge is only unlocked by actual quest state. Action Movie one-liners sit outside the personality tree.
