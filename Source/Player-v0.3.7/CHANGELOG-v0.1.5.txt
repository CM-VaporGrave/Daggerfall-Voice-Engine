Daggerfall Narrator - Player v0.1.5

- Shrinks keyboard speech to a compact single-line parchment input and horizontally scrolls long typed text so the current text remains visible.
- Adds location-first bark resolution. Exact location pools take priority over regional, guild/reputation, race/personality, and generic pools.
- Adds region, guild membership/title, and local legal-reputation context to the bark resolver and template variables.
- Adds authored pools for Daggerfall, Wayrest, Sentinel, Privateer's Hold and several major locations, plus guild/reputation reactions.
- Location transitions can now trigger contextual barks even when the broad context remains TownEnter.
- Barks.json schema v3 merges the new contextual pools into existing generated libraries without requiring deletion.
