# Daggerfall Narrator - Player - Writing Bible

## Voice
PlayerVO should sound native to Tamriel in Daggerfall's own period. The tonal guide is Morrowind's restraint: concise, culturally specific, slightly formal when appropriate, and willing to let profession, race, religion, geography, and prejudice establish worldview without turning every line into exposition.

## Time boundary
The default player voice knows the Iliac Bay as it exists during Daggerfall. Do not write as though later Third Era outcomes are already historical facts. Main-quest secrets are only referenced from explicit quest-state keys, and those lines should remain spoiler-light.

## Race
Race adds authored alternatives and cultural point of view. It is not a mechanical accent rewrite and should not reduce a race to a single joke. Khajiit may use established third-person constructions; Altmer can show refinement and superiority; Redguards can emphasize discipline, honor, and practiced skill; Bretons can sound at home in the Bay's guild/political culture; Nords can be blunt and martial; Dunmer dry and severe; Bosmer trail-aware; Argonians adaptive and restrained.

## Personality
Personality is the root of authored bark selection and is independent of both voice and race. Each Birthsign archetype has a governing worldview and lore domain. The Apprentice maps to the Agent: new to the land, a quiet learner dedicated to the larger quest, and deliberately sparing with words. The Agent declines ambient observation chatter but can speak for deliberate choices and a small set of mission-critical automatic events.

Mirror pairs are intentional: Mage/Ritual (responsible knowledge vs forbidden power), Lady/Lord (stewardship vs domination), Warrior/Thief (honorable action vs unfair advantage), Lover/Serpent (compassion vs cruelty), Steed/Tower (adventure vs possession), and Atronach/Shadow (professional confidence vs suspicious bounty-hunter vigilance).

## Player authorship
Typed keyboard speech is never rewritten. Normal Ask/Where Is/Tell Me About questions are spoken exactly as supplied by the host UI. Semantic quest/court responses may use authored equivalents only after the player has already selected the authoritative vanilla choice.

## Action Movie
Action Movie is the explicit exception to period voice. Movie-style references are allowed only in Action.* pools and only when the triggering context fits. Prefer lore-friendly adaptations where the joke survives them.

## Performance
Barks should normally be one short sentence. Short lines synthesize faster, cache well, and are less likely to collide with native player sounds or NPC dialogue.

## Context priority
Personality determines **why** the character cares; context determines **what** they are reacting to; lore profile determines **what they plausibly know**; race contributes cultural perspective. Within a birthsign, prefer event-specific exact location, region, active affiliation/guild, local reputation, and race/culture before broader personality fallbacks. General location/lore flavor is reserved for place-oriented or manual contexts so it never displaces urgent combat, health, or survival reactions.

Guild and reputation lines should acknowledge standing without inventing quest facts. Specific location lines may reference established public history or atmosphere, but Main Quest secrets are only unlocked by explicit quest-state keys. Action Movie lines are the only deliberate anachronistic one-liner layer.
